# Project Brief: Hyoseo Lee Portfolio & Digital Library

## 1. Vision & Identity
**Tagline:** "Be happy • Be unique • Be useful."
**Objective:** Create a digital home for a CS student that balances technical expertise with philosophical depth. The site should feel like a "well-lit room with one good book on the table"—warm, intentional, and lived-in.

### Design Principles
- **Be Happy (Warmth):** Use a palette of creams, warm sands, and terracotta. Soft geometry (rounded corners) and generous whitespace. Light mode only.
- **Be Unique (Character):** High-contrast serif typography (Newsreader) and subtle textures. Avoid generic SaaS or corporate templates.
- **Be Useful (Clarity):** Clean information hierarchy and high readability. Every element must serve a purpose.

---

## 2. Information Architecture
### Core Pages
- **Home:** A welcoming entry point featuring a bio snapshot, "Recent Thoughts," and "Currently Exploring" sections.
- **About:** A "continuous draft" of bio, technical skills (The Toolbox), and academic/professional milestones.
- **Projects:** A gallery of technical work under the "Philosophy of Code" banner, emphasizing craft over just logic.
- **Blog (Writing & Thinking):** A categorized archive for technical logs (Dev Log), brain dumps, LeetCode solutions, and TILs.
- **Thinking (Philosophical Notes):** A space for shorter, more abstract reflections.
- **Interactive Knowledge Graph:** A visual representation of the interconnected nature of the author's ideas (Planned).

---

## 3. Design System: Lived-In Minimalism
- **Typography:** 
  - Headings: *Newsreader* (Serif)
  - Body: *Inter* or similar clean Sans-Serif
- **Color Palette:**
  - Background: `#fbf9f1` (Cream)
  - Primary Accent: `#d67d5c` (Terracotta)
  - Surfaces: `#f5f4ec` / `#eeebe0`
- **Spacing:** Relaxed, allowing content to breathe.

---

## 4. Technical Requirements
- **Responsive:** Optimized for Desktop and Mobile.
- **Performance:** Fast loading with a focus on typography and optimized image assets.
- **Accessibility:** High contrast for readability, semantic HTML.
